vijay

Vijay (Joseph Vijay Chandrasekhar, born 22 June 1974) is an Indian actor‑turned‑politician who became the 9th Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu in May 2026, according to his Wikipedia page. Overview Vijay, also known as C. Joseph Vijay, is a prominent Indian figure who transitioned from a highly successful Tamil cinema career into politics. He founded the political party Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) in 2024 and assumed office as Chief Minister on 10 May 2026.
